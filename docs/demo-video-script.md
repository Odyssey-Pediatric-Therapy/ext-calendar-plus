# Calendar Plus demo video script

**Approx. 3–4 minutes**

## Introduction

**On screen:** Open the EspoCRM calendar and show the standard Month view.

**Voiceover:**  
“Calendar Plus enhances EspoCRM’s calendar to make scheduling easier and more efficient. It adds useful details and controls to the Month, Week, Day, and Timeline views, helping you manage events with fewer clicks.”

## Easier calendar navigation

**On screen:** Point out the single calendar selector and refresh control. Switch between Month, Week, Day, and Timeline.

**Voiceover:**  
“The calendar selector brings the different calendar views together in one place. And whenever you need the latest schedule, the refresh control is available in every view.”

## Event details and time slots

**On screen:** Hover over a meeting, then a task. Show attendee response indicators and collaborator names. Open calendar preferences and select a shorter slot duration; show dotted minor gridlines in Day or Week.

**Voiceover:**  
“Hover over an event to see helpful details without opening the record. Meetings can show attendees and their responses: a green check for accepted, a red X for declined, and a question mark for tentative or unanswered. Tasks show their collaborators when available.”

“For more precise scheduling, choose 5-, 10-, 15-, or 30-minute slots. Day and Week views show subtle dotted gridlines between the hour markers to make those intervals easier to read.”

## Create a calendar for your team

**On screen:** Use the selector to create a custom Timeline calendar. Name it “Renewal Planning” and select the Product Development team plus Olivia as an individual user. Save and show the timeline.

**Voiceover:**  
“EspoCRM includes standard Month, Week, Day, and Timeline views. Calendar Plus also lets you create custom Timeline calendars. Combine whole teams with specific people—for example, the Product Development team and one subcontractor—in a single view.”

“Team membership is resolved as the calendar fetches events, including when you refresh or change dates. That keeps the view current and leaves inactive or deleted users out of the roster.”

## A clearer shared schedule

**On screen:** Open a custom Day calendar with six sales users. Hover over one person in the legend, then hide and show a couple of people. Switch to Week mode.

**Voiceover:**  
“Custom Day, Week, and Month calendars add tools to help you focus on the schedule. In Day, each person has an aligned column. Hover over a person to highlight their events, or select people in the legend to show and hide their schedules. These visibility choices are remembered for each calendar.”

“The same focus controls work in Week, where events share the week’s day columns.”

**On screen:** Point out the colors on several events and, if enabled, the gradient in Month or Week.

**Voiceover:**  
“Stable colors help distinguish people. In Month and Week, an optional gradient keeps most of each event in its usual activity color, with the person’s color in the upper-left corner. Day and Timeline use solid activity colors.”

## Invite attendees, add collaborators, or reassign

**On screen:** In Day, drag the renewal planning meeting—which does not currently include Olivia—to Olivia’s column. Then demonstrate the same action in Week by dropping a meeting onto Olivia’s legend entry. Show Olivia added to the attendee list. Next, drag an event that supports collaborators onto another user and show them added as a collaborator. Finally, drag an event that supports neither attendees nor collaborators onto another user to demonstrate reassignment.

**Voiceover:**  
“A single drag-and-drop gesture can do three things, depending on the event. Calendar Plus adds the person as an attendee when the event supports attendees; otherwise, it adds them as a collaborator when supported; and if neither is supported, it reassigns the event. Existing participants and their response statuses stay intact.”

## Timeline settings

**On screen:** Show back-to-back events sharing a Timeline row and overlapping events on separate rows. Open Administration → Extension Settings → Calendar Plus and show Timeline spacing.

**Voiceover:**  
“Timeline rows are compact by default: back-to-back events can share a row, while genuine overlaps still stack. Administrators can adjust the spacing, including restoring the original gap if preferred.”

## Administration and close

**On screen:** Show the tooltip and delay settings, task all-day setting, Month/Week gradient setting, and Timeline spacing setting. Turn off the task all-day option and show tasks in the timed area; turn it back on and show them in the all-day row. Return to the shared calendar.

**Voiceover:**  
“Administrators can also control whether event tooltips are shown, how long they take to appear, whether tasks appear as all-day events, and whether Month and Week use color gradients. The task option changes only how tasks are displayed in the calendar; it does not change task records or system metadata. Turn it back on to restore tasks to the all-day row.”

“These options make it easy to tune Calendar Plus for your team.”

“Thanks for considering Calendar Plus for EspoCRM. If you have questions or suggestions, please let us know.”

## Demo prep

- Add sample users, a team, and a custom Timeline calendar.
- Include meetings with accepted, declined, tentative, and unanswered attendees.
- Add a task with collaborators, several short events, and all-day events.
- Set up a custom Day calendar with several users and a custom Week calendar.
- Add back-to-back and overlapping Timeline events to show row behavior.
